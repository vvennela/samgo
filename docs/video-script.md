# SAMgo demo: rough script (in Vishnu's voice), v8 order

0:00 Intro (on camera): "We all know models can draw anything you like. But can they build valid shapes that can exist
in the real world? ... Introducing SAMgo."  [the two Muse Image pictures, labelled "Generated by Meta Muse Image"]

0:12 The drawing, marked up (VO): "These look like LEGO. They're not. The plates bend, the bricks curve, the doors have
no hinges, and nothing tells you what holds the mirror on. There's no parts list, so there's nothing to check."

0:24 What existed before (on camera): "Everything you're about to see, I built this week, on Meta's models: SAM 3.1,
SAM 3D, Muse Spark, and Muse Glimmer, which I fine-tuned."

0:32 What SAMgo does (VO): "SAMgo takes one photo, and builds the whole thing out of real LEGO pieces. At full size,
that's 140,493 pieces, every one connected. Nobody's building that. So we scale it down to 293 pieces, and it keeps
its shape."

0:52 Making it work (VO): "But a shape isn't a car. Muse Spark looks at the photo and knows what a car does: wheels
here, doors here. Muse Glimmer adds the axles and wheels piece by piece, and a checker tests every single piece. Does
it connect? Does it turn? Does it stand?"

1:15 The real run (VO): "This is a real run, every model call as it happened. SAM 3.1 finds the car and its glass.
SAM 3D turns one photo into a 3D shape. Spark says where the wheels and doors go. Glimmer builds the axles and
wheels, and the checker signs off on every piece."  (the recorded run ends at the wheels; don't say it built doors)

1:58 The manual (VO or hands with the printed pages): "And it comes with instructions. Fifty-six steps, every one
checked to hold. These are real instructions. You could build this tonight."

2:07 Generalizes (VO over the tour): "And SAMgo generalizes really well. A 747. A helicopter. A Cessna. None of them
were in the training data. Same models, same checker. A bed, an office chair, a skateboard, a TV."

2:35 Why it matters (on camera): "A lot of manufacturing is assembling known parts, and people still work out how they
go together by hand. SAMgo does that for LEGO today. Next, I want to do it with real CAD parts."

2:52 Close: "SAMgo. One photo, a machine you can actually build."

Accuracy notes: cars were a training family, so no "never trained on" over the supercar or the Camry.
